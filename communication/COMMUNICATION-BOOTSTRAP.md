# Bootstrap communication

## Purpose

Create the first useful `COMMUNICATION.md` from representative evidence,
explicit goals, and user-reviewed calibration. This is normally a one-time,
manually triggered job, but it must be safe to rerun intentionally.

## Inputs and boundaries

Before retrieval, establish with the user:

- which sources and accounts are in scope;
- the time range and any people, channels, or topics to exclude;
- where retrieved material may be cached and for how long;
- current communication goals and desired areas of growth;
- the difference between drafting and external-action authority; and
- the amount and form of review the user is willing to provide.

Prefer a bounded, representative corpus over exhaustive history. Separate
messages authored by the user from received messages and quoted material.
Preserve source provenance. Store retrieved or derived runtime material only
under `.galaga/`.

## Workflow

1. Read `COMMUNICATION-SPEC.md` and, if it exists, the current
   `COMMUNICATION.md`. Absence means this pillar has not been bootstrapped.
2. Gather representative evidence across available mediums, relationships,
   goals, and situations. Reuse valid cached material instead of retrieving or
   processing it again.
3. Identify candidate principles, contextual variations, tensions, desired
   changes, and gaps. Do not convert isolated behavior into a universal rule.
4. Select candidate writing samples for the distinct behaviors they
   demonstrate. Sanitize details that are unnecessary to the lesson.
5. Present concise findings and samples to the user. Ask the user to accept,
   reject, edit, contextualize, or mark them as behavior to avoid.
6. Ask focused questions only for ambiguities that could materially change
   future communication.
7. Generate realistic calibration scenarios that cover both communication
   decisions and execution. Include underrepresented contexts when useful.
8. Have the user evaluate or edit the recommendations and writing.
9. Create a compact `COMMUNICATION.md` from endorsed guidance and samples.
   Preserve meaningful uncertainty and areas that remain unknown.
10. Evaluate the artifact against `COMMUNICATION-SPEC.md` and repair supported
    deficiencies without inventing evidence.

User review is a required part of bootstrap. Do not label an observed or
generated sample authoritative merely because it appears representative.

## Output

The output is a user-owned `COMMUNICATION.md` that improves both decide and act
performance. Report:

- sources and time ranges used;
- important exclusions and limitations;
- what the user explicitly endorsed;
- unresolved gaps or uncertainty; and
- the behavioral evaluation performed.

Do not activate scheduled jobs or external actions without the user's chosen
cadence, permissions, and notification behavior.

## Completion criteria

Bootstrap is complete when:

- the user has reviewed the material that became authoritative;
- the artifact satisfies the semantic requirements of
  `COMMUNICATION-SPEC.md`;
- representative behavioral checks show a useful improvement;
- uncertainty and missing coverage are visible; and
- no private source material has entered a framework file.
